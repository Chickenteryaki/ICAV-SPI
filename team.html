Who we are

Our team
at Imperial.

Around 20 of us work across aerodynamics, structures, electronics and flight operations. We take the aircraft from design reviews and workshop assembly to the flying field.

Working together

From review
to flight.

We compare the aircraft’s behaviour in flight with our predictions, then take the findings back to the workshop. Launch procedures, controls and the airframe all form part of that review.

How we work

Our subteams.

Each subteam owns a different part of the project. Design reviews, workshop sessions and flight days bring that work together.

01

Aerodynamics

We study wing shape and flexibility, using performance analysis and aeroelastic models to inform the design.

02

Structures

We design and make the airframe, balancing weight and stiffness with practical assembly and transport.

03

Electronics + Controls

We bring together power systems, onboard electronics and flight controls, and record data from the aircraft.

04

Flight Operations

We plan test flights, prepare launch procedures and review the aircraft’s performance afterwards.

05

Outreach

We share the project at events and build relationships with people and organisations that can support the work.
