We are ICAV SPI

Built by us.
Flown by us.

We’re students from across Imperial, working together to build an aircraft that can fly through the day and night using solar energy and batteries.

ICAV SPI · In the workshop

Who we are

Our team
at Imperial.

There are around 20 of us, working on aerodynamics, structures, electronics, controls, manufacturing, flight testing and outreach.

We learn by making things and testing them. A part we design in the workshop has to work on the aircraft, and every test gives us something to improve.

Beyond the workshop

Built in the workshop.
Tested in the field.

Getting the aircraft to a test is a team effort too. We carry it across London, assemble it on site and work through the checks together before it leaves the ground.

Working together

From review
to flight.

We meet to compare results, work through problems and decide what to test next. Changes to one part of the aircraft affect the others, so we keep those decisions shared.

How we work

How we build
our aircraft.

Each part of the team has a different job. We work closely together because weight, power and flight performance all depend on each other.

01

Aerodynamics

We design the wing and model how it flies, looking for ways to reduce drag and use less power.

02

Structures

We design the wings and airframe to be light, strong and practical to build and transport.

03

Electronics + Controls

We connect the power system, sensors and flight controls, and make sure we can monitor the aircraft from the ground.

04

Flight Operations

We prepare the aircraft, carry out flight tests and bring the results back to the workshop.

05

Outreach

We take the aircraft to events, explain what we’re building and stay in touch with our supporters.
