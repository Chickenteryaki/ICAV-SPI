The people behind the aircraft

One team.
Many disciplines.

Around 20 students across aerodynamics, structures, electronics, controls, manufacturing, flight testing and outreach.

Real project photograph · ICAV-SPI LinkedIn

Built by students.
Run like a flight programme.

ICAV-SPI brings together students from multiple engineering disciplines to design, manufacture, integrate and operate real solar-capable aircraft.

How the team works
01

Aerodynamics

Wing design, performance modelling, aeroelastic behaviour and the power required to stay aloft.

02

Structures + Manufacturing

Lightweight spars, skins, joints, composite and printed components, assembly and repair.

03

Electronics + Controls

Power systems, avionics, telemetry, flight controls and autonomous operation.

04

Flight Operations

Transport, field assembly, launch procedures, testing, data review and operational learning.

05

Outreach

Public engagement, sponsor relationships and sharing sustainable aviation with future engineers.
