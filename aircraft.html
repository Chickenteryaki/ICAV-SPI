Every watt.

The programme develops lightweight unmanned aircraft around one systems-level problem: staying airborne for longer than the current sub-50 kg endurance benchmark.

From demonstrator
to record aircraft.

The January 2026 demonstrator was designed to validate the team’s methodology, launch procedure and flight-control assumptions before committing those lessons to a larger endurance platform.

Felix reported a 4.2 m wingspan, an 8 kg mass, aluminium and carbon-fibre spars, balsa skin and 3D-printed components. The aircraft was battery-powered for the test but designed to accommodate wing-mounted solar cells.

Engineering the endurance
01

Aerodynamics

High-aspect-ratio wings, low-drag optimisation and aeroelastic modelling reduce the continuous power required for level flight.

02

Structures

Lightweight spars, skins and joints must remain stiff enough for a large wing while surviving transport, assembly and repeated flight testing.

03

Solar + Storage

Daytime solar generation must power the aircraft and replenish batteries for night operation, making energy balance a 24-hour design problem.

04

Flight Controls

Avionics and control laws have to maintain efficient, reliable flight across long missions while providing useful telemetry for engineering decisions.
