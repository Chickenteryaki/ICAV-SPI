We are ICAV SPI

From our workshop
to the open sky.

We’re students at Imperial College London designing, building and testing unmanned aircraft. Our goal is a solar-powered aircraft that can stay in the air for several days.

That means making a light, efficient airframe, collecting enough energy during the day and storing enough to fly through the night. We’re working towards it one prototype and one flight test at a time.
